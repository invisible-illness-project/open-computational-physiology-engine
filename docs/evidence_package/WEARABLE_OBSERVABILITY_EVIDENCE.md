# OCPE — Wearable Observability Evidence Map
**Scope:** consumer/research wearables (wrist/finger PPG, ECG patches, IMU, EDA, skin temperature, CGM, SpO2) as simulation targets for OCPE synthetic datasets of systemic disorders (POTS, ME/CFS, long COVID, EDS, autoimmune, metabolic, neurological).
**Purpose:** For every physiological variable in scope, classify observability and document the demonstrated accuracy/error evidence so the engine never fabricates observable signals for latent variables.

## Evidence-level rubric (E0–E5) applied to every claim
- **E0** — Established clinical/physiological standard; direct measurement with well-characterized metrology.
- **E1** — Demonstrated inference: experimentally validated against a reference with published accuracy statistics (MAE/LoA/r/AUC/κ/sens/spec) in independent or multi-cohort studies.
- **E2** — Demonstrated in limited settings: single studies, small/healthy/homogeneous samples, or feasibility-only; accuracy stats exist but generalization unproven.
- **E3** — Plausible: physiological mechanism documented, but no (or no meaningful) quantitative inference accuracy for wearable conditions.
- **E4** — Speculative / marketing claim: commercially asserted but contradicted, unvalidated, or failing validation standards in independent testing.
- **E5** — Background/consensus physiology (textbook-level mechanism statements).

**Verdicts:** `direct` (the variable itself is the measured quantity) · `inferred` (validated estimator with quantified error) · `plausible` (mechanism only; engine may emit the *affected signal*, never the latent value) · `latent-only` (must never appear as a direct wearable channel).

## (b) OBSERVABILITY CHAINS (non-trivial cases)

Format: **latent physiology → mechanism → observable quantity → sensor modality → raw waveform → derived feature** | sampling | error | confounders | mimics.

### C2. HRV / vagal activity (workhorse autonomic channel)
Vagal (and baroreflex) modulation of the sinoatrial node → beat-to-beat RR-interval variation → pulse-arrival intervals at periphery → reflectance PPG (green 525 nm / IR) at wrist or finger → raw PPG waveform (AC pulsatile component) → pulse-peak times → PRV series → RMSSD / SDNN / HF power.
- **Sampling:** raw PPG ≥100 Hz desirable; 25 Hz degrades peak interpolation unless upsampled (parabolic interpolation recovers much). Windows: RMSSD valid at 1–5 min; 10-s ultra-short r≈0.85 only if averaged over ≥3 segments.
- **Error:** rest supine bias −2 to −8 ms vs ECG, ICC 0.96–0.98; PRV inflates RMSSD ~3–6% and distorts LF/HF by 10–40% during sympathetic activation; posture transitions and exercise degrade to ICC ~0.5–0.6.
- **Confounders:** respiration rate/depth (RSA enters HF band), age, posture, time of day, caffeine, beta-blockers.
- **Mimics:** motion spikes read as ectopy → false RMSSD elevation; missed peaks → false RMSSD suppression. **OCPE rule:** simulated PRV must include pulse-transit-time jitter + motion-dependent dropout, and PRV≠HRV divergence under autonomic transients.

### C3. Cuffless BP
LV ejection → pressure wave propagates at velocity set by arterial wall stiffness (Bramwell–Hill/Moens–Korteweg: PWV ∝ √(E·h/ρ·d)) → pulse transit/arrival time (PTT/PAT) and PPG waveform morphology change with BP → ECG R-peak + PPG foot (PAT) or dual-site PPG → raw time-aligned waveforms → PAT per beat / waveform features → BP estimate via calibrated regression.
- **Sampling:** ≥125–250 Hz both channels for stable PAT.
- **Error (demonstrated):** passes IEEE 1708 (MAD ≤7 mmHg) and sometimes ISO criterion 1 **only near the calibration point, in normotensive cohorts**; LoA ±16 mmHg (SBP, GW5 28-day); MD grows to 3.4/5.1 mmHg at ±10 mmHg from calibration; GW Active 2 failed ISO outright (SD 15.5/22.5 mmHg); calibration drift 0.022 mmHg/day after day 15.
- **Confounders:** hydrostatic wrist height (~0.7 mmHg/cm), vasomotor state, arterial stiffness (age, diabetes), cuff calibration error propagated forever.
- **Mimics:** a PTT change from vasoconstriction (cold hands) is indistinguishable from a BP change.
- **OCPE rule:** emit BP only as *calibration-anchored trend with distance-from-calibration error growth*; absolute BP is not observable. For POTS/orthostatic disorders, note POTS diagnostic criteria explicitly EXCLUDE BP drop — cuffless BP cannot certify "no orthostatic hypotension."

### C4. Stroke volume / cardiac output
LV contraction → whole-body recoil (ballistic forces) → body micron-acceleration → BCG (force plate/scale/armband accelerometer; NOT in consumer wrist devices) → IJK-wave complexes → J-wave amplitude/timing features → SV/CO regression.
- **Error:** CO MAE 0.83 L/min, PE 30.2% (at the interchangeability limit); regression toward mean CO; ΔSV tracking error ~5.3% under controlled recovery.
- **OCPE rule:** for wrist-worn targets, SV and CO are **latent-only**: they may modulate PPG pulse amplitude and BCG-free features plausibly (E3) but no validated wrist estimator exists. Model their effect *on* PPG amplitude (confounded by local vasomotion) rather than emitting an SV channel.

### R1. Respiration rate from PPG
Breathing → intrathoracic pressure swings + RSA → baseline wander (RIIV), amplitude (RIAV) and frequency (RIFV) modulation of PPG → raw PPG → three respiratory-modulated surrogate series → spectral/fusion RR estimate.
- **Sampling:** PPG ≥25 Hz; 32–90 s windows.
- **Error:** MAE 0.1–2.3 br/min in clinical datasets; realistically ±2 br/min at rest; fails during motion, talking, irregular breathing.
- **Mimics:** 0.1 Hz Mayer waves, slow movement, HR at respiratory alias frequencies.

### T2/T3. Core temperature and fever
Hypothalamic set-point → skin vasomotor drive + metabolic heat → distal skin temperature tracks core *only loosely* (gradient set by ambient conditions and vasomotor state) → ring/wrist thermistor → skin temp time series → (i) direct skin temp; (ii) core estimate via heat-balance/Kalman using HR as metabolic proxy (ECTemp-class); (iii) fever flag via personal-baseline deviation.
- **Error:** exertional core estimation MAE ≈0.3 °C in heat-stress cohorts (E2); resting/febrile core estimation from wrist/ring unvalidated (E3–E4). Fever *deviation* detection: +0.63 °C mean elevation, 93% pre-symptomatic anomaly rate in 50 COVID cases (E2).
- **Confounders/mimics:** ambient temperature, bedding, alcohol, ovulation (biphasic +0.3 °C), menopause flashes.

### AC3. Sleep staging
Cortical state (latent) → autonomic + motor correlates (HR↓, HRV changes, immobility, distal vasodilation/temperature) → PPG + IMU (+temp) → epoch features → proprietary classifier → wake/light/deep/REM.
- **Error:** sleep/wake sens ≥95%; 4-stage κ 0.37–0.65 vs human inter-rater 0.76; deep & REM nightly-duration ICC ≤0.37 — stage *minutes* are unreliable even when epoch accuracy looks acceptable.
- **OCPE rule:** synthetic staging output must carry per-stage confusion matrices (deep↔light, REM↔light), wake-immobility false sleeps, and device-specific bias (e.g., Fitbit overestimates light ~18 min, underestimates deep ~15 min).

### S1. Orthostatic episode (POTS-relevant)
Venous pooling on standing (latent) → venous return↓ → transient SV/CO↓ → baroreflex → sympathetic↑/vagal↓ → HR surge (≥30 bpm within 10 min = POTS criterion) → IMU posture-transition event + HR time course → ΔHR-from-pre-standing-baseline feature.
- **Evidence:** pilot-scale only (n=7 hEDS, 8 controls, Corsano watch): standing events detectable, ΔHR highly variable even within group (6.6–27 bpm in CONTROLS) → specificity of a naive ΔHR detector is poor.
- **Confounders/mimics:** speed of rising, prior rest duration, caffeine, hydration, meals, warm environment, anxiety on standing. This is why the 10-min sustained criterion and posture context are mandatory in the model.

### S2. PEM episode
Exertion beyond threshold → (latent pathophysiology: immune/metabolic) → delayed (12–48 h) symptom flare → hypothesized correlates: nighttime RMSSD suppression (demonstrated at group level after VT1+ exercise in long COVID), resting-HR elevation, activity collapse (pacing) → daily-aggregate anomaly detection.
- **Evidence status:** NO validated individual PEM detector (E3 for individual detection; E2 for group HRV-after-exertion effect). Activity-matching abolishes most apparent group differences — the dominant confounder is behavioral adaptation (pacing), not physiology.
- **OCPE rule:** PEM may only modulate *distribution shifts* in overnight RMSSD / RHR / next-day activity with explicitly low effect sizes and high false-alarm rates; never emit a "PEM detected" ground-truth-matching channel.

### S4. Infection onset
Immune response (latent) → cytokine-mediated autonomic shift → RHR↑, HRV↓, sleep fragmentation, distal temp↑ → personal-baseline residuals → anomaly alert.
- **Error/AUCs:** RHR alone AUC 0.52 (useless); multimodal passive AUC 0.70–0.72; with symptoms 0.80; meta-analytic sens ~80% / spec ~77%. Pre-symptomatic flag rate ~63% (retrospective).
- **Mimics:** alcohol, travel/jet lag, hard training, stress, any other infection — non-specificity is intrinsic.

## (c) DEMONSTRATED vs CLAIMED — marketing-vs-evidence gaps

1. **Cuffless BP (biggest gap).** Claimed: "BP on your wrist" (regional clearances for Samsung/Huawei features). Demonstrated: passes IEEE 1708 MAD ≤7 mmHg only when cuff-calibrated and near the calibration BP in mostly young normotensives; independent 24-h ABPM study failed ISO (SD 15.5/22.5 mmHg); bias regresses to the calibration point; drift ~6.8 mmHg over a calibration cycle. Korean Hypertension Society position paper: not for clinical decisions, discrepancy worst exactly where it matters (≥160 / ≤60 mmHg). **No FDA clearance exists for any calibration-free consumer wrist BP.** Gap category: *trend feature marketed as measurement*.
2. **Optical noninvasive glucose.** Claimed repeatedly by startups and smartwatch rumors. Demonstrated: no validated wrist-optical glucose; the only accurate wearable glucose is minimally invasive enzymatic CGM (MARD 7.9–8.2%). A 2025 conference abstract claiming MARD 8.8% with visible light is unvalidated. Gap category: *latent variable marketed as observable*.
3. **Hydration.** Claimed by multiple consumer products. Demonstrated: proxies (bioimpedance TBW ±3.2%, NIRS r≈0.79 acute) exist in controlled settings; every proxy is dominated by temperature/ambient/recent-intake confounders; no device meets clinical thresholds; key ML result is single-subject. Gap category: *behavioral nudge marketed as physiological measurement*.
4. **Stress scores.** Claimed: continuous "stress 0–100." Demonstrated: lab stressors classifiable at ~80–93% only within-dataset with EDA+HR; systematic reviews show no cross-dataset/real-world generalization; EDA and IBI degrade severely with motion. What wearables actually measure is *autonomic arousal*, which is valence- and cause-blind. Gap category: *arousal marketed as psychological stress*.
5. **Core temperature.** Claimed: "body temperature" on rings/watches. Demonstrated: accurate skin temperature; core estimation validated only for exertional heat-stress models (MAE ~0.3 °C) in fit young cohorts; resting core and fever °C from a wrist/ring unvalidated. TemPredict-style results detect *relative deviation*, never absolute fever.
6. **Sleep stages.** Claimed: precise deep/REM minutes. Demonstrated: κ 0.37–0.65; deep/REM nightly durations ICC ≤0.37; only sleep/wake and TST are trustworthy. Gap category: *staging marketed as PSG-equivalent*.
7. **Cerebral perfusion / "brain oxygen."** Occasionally implied by wellness devices; no wrist inference exists; only head-worn NIRS (research/clinical) touches it. Latent-only.
8. **SpO2 (partial gap).** Best devices meet ISO reflectance criteria even in controlled hypoxia (bias ~0, LoA ±6%); several popular devices show 5–13% bias at low saturation. Claims exceed evidence device-by-device — validate per model, not per modality.

## (d) IMPLICATIONS FOR OCPE

### Latent variables that must NEVER emit a direct wearable signal
- **Absolute blood pressure** (only calibration-anchored, error-growing trend), **systemic vascular resistance**, **stroke volume / cardiac output** (wrist), **venous pooling volume**, **cerebral perfusion**, **core temperature in °C (resting/febrile)**, **hydration status**, **blood glucose (any optical channel)**, **baroreflex sensitivity in ms/mmHg**, **PEM episodes**, **psychological stress as ground truth**.
- These variables may **modulate observable signals** through documented mechanisms (e.g., venous pooling → postural ΔHR; SV → PPG amplitude; core temp → skin temp + HR; glucose → nothing optical) — the engine should implement the *coupling*, not the *readout*.

### Inferred features that MUST carry modeled inference error
| Feature | Error model to implement |
|---|---|
| PRV/HRV from PPG | +3–6% RMSSD inflation; motion-dependent dropout; posture-transition ICC degradation; age/sex-dependent LoA widening |
| Cuffless BP trend | Bias toward calibration point; error growth ∝ distance from calibration; ~0.02 mmHg/day drift; occasional ISO-failing devices (SD up to 22 mmHg) |
| SpO2 | Bias 0–1%, LoA ±6%; bias inflation below 90% (device-dependent up to 13%); motion/low-perfusion data voids |
| RR from PPG | ±2 br/min at rest; motion-band aliasing; window-size dependence |
| Sleep stages | Per-stage confusion matrix; wake-immobility false positives; deep/REM duration ICC ≤0.37; device-specific bias offsets |
| Energy expenditure | Heteroscedastic error commonly ±20–30% individual; systematic underestimate ~11% median free-living |
| Steps | MAPE 1–3% normal gait → 5–30% in slow/intermittent/pathological gait (critical for ME/CFS & neurological cohorts) |
| Stress/arousal | Classifier outputs as probability of lab-stressor-like arousal, with motion-corruption and no ecological validity |
| Infection/illness deviation | AUC ~0.7 passive-only; high false-alarm rate at low prevalence; non-specificity to cause |
| Core temp estimate | ±0.3 °C only under exertional-heat model conditions; undefined error at rest/fever → restrict to skin temp + deviation flags |

### Design rules
1. **Separation of latent state and observation layer:** every wearable channel = f(latent physiology, sensor physics, motion/artifact model, inference algorithm with its published error distribution). No direct latent→channel passthrough except HR, skin temp, IMU counts, CGM glucose, SpO2 (within validated envelope).
2. **Disease-context validity flags:** HR-based metrics break specifically in the target populations (POTS tachycardia decouples HR from intensity; ME/CFS pacing invalidates activity-EE links; dysautonomia violates PRV≈HRV assumptions during orthostatic transients). Synthetic datasets must include these broken-sensor regimes, not just nominal ones.
3. **Artifact mimics are mandatory:** for every inferred feature, implement its documented mimics (motion-as-HRV, immobility-as-sleep, cold-vasoconstriction-as-BP-change, slow gait undercounting, alcohol/jet-lag-as-infection-anomaly).
4. **Sampling-rate fidelity:** PPG ≥25 Hz (100 Hz for HRV/PTT features), EDA ≥4 Hz, IMU ≥25 Hz, temperature ~1 sample/min; inference-window durations (RR 32–90 s, HRV 1–5 min, sleep 30-s epochs) are part of the observation model.
5. **Ground-truth retention:** synthetic datasets should ship both the true latent trajectories AND the noisy observed channels, with the inference layer versioned separately — enabling benchmark of "how much of POTS/PEM/long-COVID pathophysiology is actually visible at the wrist."

### Evidence gaps flagged for future passes
- No validated individual PEM detector (largest OCPE-relevant gap).
- Orthostatic ΔHR wearables: pilot-scale only (n≤19); no sensitivity/specificity vs tilt-table exists.
- Core-temp estimation in febrile/resting states unvalidated.
- EDA↔MSNA quantitative link established mechanistically but wearable-condition accuracy stats sparse.
- Cerebral perfusion: zero wrist inference literature.
